Research overview

The code simulates a bi-static Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) system for passive sensing of multiple vehicles. It includes forward-backward smoothing for snapshot augmentation, SWIP-MUSIC combining periodogram and MUSIC methods for time-offset estimation, and periodogram-based joint Doppler–delay estimation.

THE APPROACH

Methods

  1. 01Snapshots

    Forward-backward smoothing

    FBS (Forward-Backward Smoothing) for snapshot augmentation.

  2. 02SWIP-MUSIC

    Time-offset estimation

    SWIP-MUSIC combines periodogram and MUSIC methods for time-offset estimation.

  3. 03Delay–Doppler

    Joint Doppler–delay estimation

    Joint Doppler–delay estimation using periodogram-based algorithms.
