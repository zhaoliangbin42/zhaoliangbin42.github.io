What can an uplink signal tell us about its surroundings?

Research overview

Hybrid arrays and unsynchronized clocks complicate uplink sensing. This work combines frequency smoothing, reference-signal construction, and AB2FM to estimate arrival angles, Doppler shifts, and delays while preserving their association.

THE APPROACH

The method, in three steps.

  1. 01AoA

    Resolve the directions

    Beam scanning and frequency smoothing resolve arrival angles for static and moving paths.

  2. 02Reference

    Handle clock offsets

    A constructed reference signal reduces the impact of unsynchronized transmitter and receiver clocks.

  3. 03AB2FM

    Estimate delay and Doppler

    AoA-based 2D-FFT-MUSIC jointly estimates Doppler and delay.
