AI-MarkDone

Feature guide

AI-MarkDone is for heavy ChatGPT users, especially researchers and STEM workers. It brings scattered copying, screenshots, exports, and annotations into a more efficient ChatGPT workflow.

Reader

Give long answers a quiet reading interface, away from distractions. Built-in tools help you move much faster: Quick message switching, navigation bar, partial copy, Sticky panel, and Dynamic Annotation.

Learn more

Formula copy/export

Take one formula directly. Copy LaTeX or export PNG, SVG, and MathML for papers, slides, and notes.

Learn more

Bookmarks

Keep the answers you will need again. Bookmarks save the content and source position, with folders, search, preview, and jump-back.
