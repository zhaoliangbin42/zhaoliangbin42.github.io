AI-MarkDone Manual

Learn AI-MarkDone usage methods and productivity techniques through readable, capture-ready, askable, and keepable ChatGPT workflows.

AI-MarkDone adds reading, copying, annotation, bookmark, and export tools to ChatGPT for working with long answers, formulas, and code.

Install the extension, refresh ChatGPT, and start from the toolbar below a message. Choose a feature from the four groups below, or begin with Installation .

AI-MarkDone’s core functions fall into four groups: readable, easy to capture, easy to ask, and easy to keep.

Readable

Read long answers in Reader, follow the heading outline, switch messages, and keep temporary excerpts in the Sticky panel.

Easy to capture

Copy a full answer or a selected passage as Markdown, take the LaTeX source of a formula, or export a formula image.

Easy to ask

Annotate selected source text and use prompt templates to prepare a follow-up question.

Easy to keep

Save answers as bookmarks and organize them into folders, or batch-export Markdown, PDF, or PNG files for sharing and reference.
