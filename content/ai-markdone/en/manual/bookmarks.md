## What this solves

Some answers are not ready to export immediately, but you know you will need them later. A browser bookmark saves the whole conversation, not the exact message.

AI-MarkDone bookmarks save specific messages and can jump back to their original ChatGPT location.

## When to use it

- Save a useful explanation.
- Collect reusable prompts or writing patterns.
- Keep a solved error and its debugging steps.
- Group related answers into folders.

## Where to open it

Save the current message from the message toolbar. Later, open the extension panel and use Bookmarks to search, preview, move, copy, or export saved items.

## Steps

1. Find the ChatGPT answer worth saving.
2. Click the bookmark action in the message toolbar.
3. Give the bookmark a clear title.
4. Choose or create a folder.
5. Preview the saved content in the Bookmarks panel.
6. Copy Markdown or jump back to the original conversation when needed.
7. Export bookmarks before uninstalling or moving browsers.

## Common questions

### What should become a bookmark?

Save material you expect to reuse. Do not save every answer. A selective bookmark library is easier to search.

### Where is the data stored?

Bookmarks stay in browser extension storage. AI-MarkDone does not run an owned server for your bookmark content.
