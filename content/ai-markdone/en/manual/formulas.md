## What this solves

Formula copy is easy to get wrong. You may need LaTeX for notes, MathML for Office, or an image asset for slides and documents.

AI-MarkDone treats formulas as their own copy target, so you do not have to manually select rendered math.

## When to use it

- You write Markdown notes and need LaTeX.
- You paste equations into Word, PowerPoint, or similar editors.
- You need PNG or SVG assets for visual documents.
- ChatGPT renders formulas inside tables.

## Where to open it

Click a formula to copy its source. Enable formula hover actions in Settings when you also need MathML, PNG, or SVG.

## Steps

1. Open a ChatGPT answer or Reader page with formulas.
2. Click a formula when you only need the source.
3. Enable hover actions in Settings for MathML, PNG, or SVG.
4. Hover over the formula.
5. Choose the copy or save action you need.
6. Paste into the target app and check the result.

## Common questions

### Which format should I choose?

Use LaTeX for Markdown notes, MathML for Office-like editors, and PNG or SVG for visual documents.

### Why are some formula actions hidden by default?

They add extra UI. AI-MarkDone keeps them off until you choose the actions you actually use.
