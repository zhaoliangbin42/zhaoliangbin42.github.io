## Use this when

Your bookmarks have become real working material, and you want a safer copy before reinstalling the browser, switching machines, or cleaning extension data.

## Where to open it

Open AI-MarkDone Settings, go to the data or backup area, choose Google Drive backup, and connect your own Google account.

## How to use it

1. Click connect Google Drive.
2. Finish the Google authorization flow.
3. Return to Settings and choose Back up now.
4. When restoring, review the backup list first, then choose safe-merge restore.

## Worth knowing

- Backup runs only after you turn it on.
- AI-MarkDone handles its own backup files.
- Export a local backup before restoring, especially if the data matters.
- If you revoke Google access, cloud backup stops. Local bookmarks are not deleted by that.
