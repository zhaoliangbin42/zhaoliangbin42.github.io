## Saved and temporary content

Bookmarks and highlights are stored in your browser. The “Save new annotations for later” setting controls whether new annotations are kept permanently. With it off, submitted annotations remain only on the current page. Excerpts are temporary and disappear when you refresh. Back up important material; see [Local backup and restore](privacy-storage/) for the scope.
