# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-06-05*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AI-MarkDone is a ChatGPT-only browser extension for serious reading, Markdown copy, LaTeX formula copy, annotations, bookmarks, export, and Google Drive bookmark backup.

**What it does:** AI-MarkDone stays on the original ChatGPT page and adds a workbench around useful answers: Reader mode, source-aware partial Markdown copy, formula copy/export, Dynamic Annotation, Sticky excerpts, bookmarks, Save Messages export, and settings that let users trim the interface. It supports Chrome and Firefox as public install paths.

**Product category:** ChatGPT browser extension, ChatGPT productivity extension, ChatGPT reader extension, ChatGPT Markdown copy extension.

**Product type:** Free and open-source browser extension.

**Business model:** Free. Primary conversion is Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons installation; GitHub stars and reviews are secondary proof/support actions.

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Heavy ChatGPT users, researchers, STEM students, graduate students, technical writers, developers, knowledge workers, and people who regularly turn ChatGPT answers into notes, documents, papers, prompts, or archives.

**Primary use case:** Turn useful ChatGPT answers into reusable material without losing source structure or leaving the conversation flow.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Read long ChatGPT answers in a calmer Reader surface.
- Copy exact passages, Markdown, formulas, code, and tables with less cleanup.
- Save, annotate, back up, and export answers for later work.

**Use cases:**
- Copy ChatGPT to Markdown for Obsidian, Typora, VS Code, Git, or technical notes.
- Copy ChatGPT formulas as LaTeX, MathML, PNG, or SVG.
- Save ChatGPT answers as bookmarks with folders, preview, search, and source jump-back.
- Export ChatGPT answers as Markdown, PDF, PNG, or multi-image ZIP.
- Back up bookmark snapshots to the user's own Google Drive.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** ChatGPT can produce useful long answers, but the original chat page is not always good for careful reading, exact copying, formula reuse, structured annotation, saving, or exporting.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Native copy can flatten formatting, lose formula structure, or include cleanup noise.
- Browser bookmarks save whole URLs, not the exact useful answer and source position.
- Screenshots, PDF export, Markdown cleanup, and note-taking often become separate manual chores.
- Asking ChatGPT to revise a long answer sentence by sentence is tedious without annotations tied to exact text.

**What it costs users:** Repeated scrolling, messy pasted content, lost answers, manual screenshot cleanup, broken formulas, and slower research or writing workflows.

**Emotional tension:** "I know ChatGPT gave me something useful, but now I have to fight the page to reuse it."

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Built around ChatGPT rather than broad multi-platform claims.
- Reader combines reading, partial copy, Sticky excerpts, annotations, message switching, and follow-up send actions.
- Formula actions handle LaTeX, MathML, PNG, and SVG without requiring full-message copy.
- Bookmarks save specific messages with folder organization, preview, source jump-back, local storage, and optional Google Drive backup.
- Free, open source, local-first, and no owned backend receiving ChatGPT content.

**Why customers choose it:** It solves the small but frequent cleanup steps around serious ChatGPT use without asking users to move their work into another app.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is it only for ChatGPT? | Yes. AI-MarkDone 4.5.0 and later use ChatGPT as the only active AI page runtime. |
| Does it upload my ChatGPT content? | No owned AI-MarkDone backend receives ChatGPT content. Optional Google Drive backup sends bookmark snapshots directly to the user's own Drive after authorization. |
| Is it overkill for casual users? | Casual users can keep defaults simple. Heavy users can enable deeper Reader, formula, export, annotation, and settings workflows. |

**Anti-persona:** Users who only send short ChatGPT prompts and never save, copy, annotate, export, or revisit answers.
